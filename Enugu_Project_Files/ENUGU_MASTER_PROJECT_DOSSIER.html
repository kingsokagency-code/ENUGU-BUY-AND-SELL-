1. Executive Summary & Product Vision

Enugu Buy & Sell is an AI-powered, social-first Student Super App & Business Engine designed specifically for university campuses, launching first at the University of Nigeria, Enugu Campus (UNEC).

Rather than building a traditional, static classifieds site (which suffers from low engagement and high scam rates), Enugu Buy & Sell combines 5 core engines:

  • High-Frequency Daily Utility: Food & grocery ordering from popular local vendors (Crunches, OpenSharaton).
  • Student Earning Opportunities: Digital storefronts for student businesses & the "Shoppers One" personal errand network.
  • Addictive Social Validation: Live view counters, likes, and love reactions to drive daily seller retention.
  • Embedded AI Intelligence: Instant flyer generation and automated copywriter tools via Google Gemini API.
  • Bank-Grade Escrow Security: Powered by Paystack HMAC verification and Supabase Row Level Security (RLS).

3. The 4 Core Strategic Growth Pillars

Visual Phased Roadmap: Phase 1 (Thin Layer MVP), Phase 2 (Shoppers One & Payments), Phase 3 (Full Logistics)

Pillar 1: Zero-Budget Viral Engine (The 2004 Harvard Model)

Facebook reached 1/3 of Harvard in 30 days without spending $1 on ads by turning users into marketers. Students get customizable digital storefronts. When they list products, they proudly share their unique shop link on WhatsApp status updates, bringing hundreds of free users daily.

Pillar 2: High-Frequency Daily Purchases ("Eat & Live")

Students don't buy laptops every day, but they eat every day. Partnering with campus food spots and supermarkets ensures Daily Active Use (DAU) as students order meals and groceries continuously.

Pillar 3: "Shoppers One" — Personal Shopping Ecosystem

Connecting busy university lecturers, workers, and wealthy locals who lack time for market runs with student runners looking to earn money. Students accept shopping tasks, buy items at local markets, and deliver them for an automated fee.

Pillar 4: Addictive Social Validation Loop (The Core MVP Hook)

Every storefront and product listing features Live View Counters ("14 students viewing now"), Likes, and Love Reactions. Instant social recognition triggers the dopamine loop that keeps sellers engaged.

4. Embedded AI Intelligence Layer (The Seller AI Copilot)

Powered by Google Gemini API, every vendor gets a built-in AI assistant to eliminate wasted time:

  • AI Flyer Studio: Generates social-media-ready promotional graphics instantly.
  • AI Copywriter: Writes catchy titles, persuasive sales copy, and search tags in 3 seconds.
  • AI Smart Pricing Assistant: Analyzes past UNEC sales to suggest optimal pricing for fast sales.
  • Background AI Scam Guard: Automatically scans product photos and text for fraud patterns before publication.

6. 5-Layer Military-Grade Security Architecture

  • Layer 1: Server-Only Key Isolation — Secret API keys run strictly inside server actions and are NEVER sent to client JavaScript.
  • Layer 2: Supabase Row Level Security (RLS) — Users can ONLY modify database records tied to their authenticated ID.
  • Layer 3: Paystack HMAC Verification — Webhooks are verified using cryptographic SHA512 signature matching.
  • Layer 4: Input Validation & XSS Defense — All payloads pass Zod schema validation; Next.js auto-escapes HTML.
  • Layer 5: Repository Hardening — Pre-commit secret scanning hooks prevent accidental credential leaks to GitHub.
